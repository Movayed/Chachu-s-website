We're the bridge between a good idea and the audience it deserves.

Founded in 2013 as a two-person buying desk, Global Communication Lmtd now plans and runs campaigns in 48 cities — with the same rule it started with: never buy a placement we can't justify.

Our story

Built by people who used to sit on the other side of the table.

Our founders spent their early careers inside broadcasters and publishing houses, watching good briefs turn into forgettable campaigns because nobody owned the whole picture. So we built the agency we wished we'd been buying from.

Today that means one strategist per account, transparent media costs, and creative produced for the exact format it will run in — whether that's a 30-second prime-time spot or a two-metre poster at a bus stop.

  • Independent — we're not owned by, or tied to, any media house.
  • Transparent — media is invoiced at cost, with the rate card attached.
  • Accountable — every campaign ends with numbers, not adjectives.

What we believe

Four rules we don't bend.

01

The plan comes before the placement

If a channel can't be tied to a goal, it isn't in the plan — however good the rate looks on the rate card.

02

Say the hard thing early

Better a difficult week two than a wasted quarter. You'll hear about the problem while there's still budget to fix it.

03

One brief, one owner

You get a named strategist and their direct line. No account layers, no call centre, no mystery about who decided what.

04

Measure what moved

Reach is not a result. We report against the number you actually care about, and we show the working.
