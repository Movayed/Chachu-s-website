How a campaign runs

Four weeks from brief to first placement.

01

Brief & audit — week 1

Goals, audience, budget and timing. We review your current spend and return a plan with expected reach and cost per thousand.

02

Creative — weeks 1–2

Assets are produced or adapted for each format, then approved against the station and publisher specs before anything is booked.

03

Buy & launch — weeks 2–3

Space is contracted, scheduled and confirmed. You get a single flight plan showing exactly where and when you appear.

04

Report & refine — week 4 onward

Weekly performance notes while the campaign is live, then a full post-campaign review with recommendations for the next flight.
